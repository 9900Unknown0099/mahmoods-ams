Explore the policy below to understand what information the app may use, why it is needed, and how to contact us about your data.

01

1. Introduction

Mahmoods AMS is an Android application designed to support academic attendance management and related academic activities for authorized students and college staff.

02

2. Information We Handle

Depending on the features used and the institution’s configuration, the application may process account information such as email addresses and account identifiers, student or staff identifiers, academic information, timetable data, and attendance records.

Firebase services may process technical information required for authentication, security, and service operation.

03

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to authenticate authorized users, provide attendance and academic features, display permitted records, synchronize supported data, and maintain application security.

04

4. Authentication and Service Providers

Mahmoods AMS uses Firebase services for supported authentication and cloud-data functions. Depending on the sign-in method, users may authenticate with email and password or Google Sign-In.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Firebase Privacy and Security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

05

5. Storage and Security

The application may store supported information locally on the device and remotely through configured cloud services. Reasonable measures are intended to protect information, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

06

6. Data Sharing

Personal and academic information is not intended to be sold for advertising purposes. Information may be processed by service providers to operate the application or disclosed when required by applicable law.

07

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information should be retained only as long as necessary for academic, operational, and legal purposes. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, contact the email address below. Requests concerning official academic records may require coordination with the responsible institution.

Uninstalling the application does not necessarily delete information stored in cloud services.

08

8. Student Information

Mahmoods AMS may process student information for academic and attendance-management purposes. Access should be limited to authorized users and their assigned permissions.

09

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when application features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this URL.
