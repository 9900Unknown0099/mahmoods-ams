Built for the classroom

Tap in. Show up. Be counted — accurately.

Manual roll calls cost teaching time and leave records open to mistakes. Mahmoods AMS records who entered, when they arrived and how long they stayed — then lets the right people see the right numbers.

One app, four roles

Everyone gets exactly what they need.

Sign in with the role assigned to your account. Students only ever see their own data; staff see the whole class.

Student

Sees only their own record — never anyone else’s.

Faculty

RFID cards · terminals · audit log

Decision engine

“Card tapped” doesn’t mean “present.”

The app measures how long a student actually stayed in class and applies the rules — so a two-second tap at the door isn’t the same as attending the lecture.

The hardware path

From a card tap to your phone.

Designed around an ESP32 with an RC522 reader at the classroom door. The app also includes a built-in RFID simulator, so every screen can be tried before hardware is installed.
