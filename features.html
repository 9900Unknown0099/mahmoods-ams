Features

Everything attendance needs.
Nothing it doesn’t.

A focused tool for one job: knowing who was in class, for how long, and making that easy for everyone to see.

For students

Your record,
honest and clear.

A home screen that answers the only question that matters: am I safe?

  • Overall percentage with an exam-eligibility status against the 75% minimum
  • Present, late, absent and short-duration counts at a glance
  • Today’s schedule with faculty and room for your own lab batch
  • Only your data — a student account can never open another student’s record

For faculty, proctors & admins

See the whole class.
Spot the gaps.

Class-wide attendance with the defaulters surfaced first.

  • Configurable threshold — switch between 75%, 70% and 65%
  • Class average and defaulter count at the top of the list
  • Search by roll number or name; filter to defaulters only
  • Per-student percentage with lectures attended out of lectures held

Timetable

A timetable
that bends.

Seeded from the official class timetable, then fully editable from inside the app.

  • Add, edit, delete slots for any day from Monday to Saturday
  • Lab batches T4, T5 and T6 with their own rooms and faculty
  • Breaks for lunch and the short break are part of the day
  • Clash checks stop two lectures from claiming the same room

Reports

Excel only.
Exactly your format.

Generate the class sheet your department already uses — in seconds.

  • Choose the period: last 5, 10 or 15 days, till date, or custom
  • Highlight students below 75%, 70% or 65%
  • Real .xlsx file with class heading, subject columns and faculty initials
  • Signature lines for Class Teacher and HOD; students can export their own report too

Sign-in

The right door
for every role.

Pick Student, Faculty, Proctor or Admin — your account has to match.

  • Email and password sign-in, with password reset
  • Google sign-in for existing accounts that have Google linked
  • Role matching: the role you choose must be the role on your account
  • Student registration straight from the sign-in screen

And the details

Small things, done properly.

RFID simulator

A “Test Tap” button runs a simulated card scan through the same attendance rules as real hardware.

Lecture timeline

Every lecture, in order, with check-in, check-out, time in class and the reason for the result.

What-if calculator

See how attending or missing upcoming lectures would change your percentage.

Corrections with reasons

Faculty can correct a record; changes are logged so nothing is silently rewritten.

Lecture swap

When another teacher takes the lecture, faculty can swap it without touching the timetable.

Audit log

Admins can see who changed what, and when.

Duplicate-scan guard

Repeated taps within a short window are ignored instead of creating extra records.

Emergency modes

Normal, check-in only, check-out only, or manual review — for when a reader misbehaves.

Lab batch awareness

Labs for batches T4, T5 and T6 only accept the students of that batch.
