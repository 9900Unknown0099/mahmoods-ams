Questions

Good to know.

Is Mahmoods AMS on Google Play?
Not yet. Version 1.4 is distributed as a direct APK from this site, which is why your phone asks for a one-time permission to install it.
Why does my phone show a warning?
Android warns about any app installed from outside the Play Store, and Google Play Protect may offer to scan it. That is expected for a direct download. To be sure you have the genuine file, compare its SHA-256 fingerprint with the one on this page.
What permissions does the app ask for?
Its manifest requests only Internet access and network-state access — no camera, contacts, location or storage permissions.
Which Android versions are supported?
Android 7.0 (Nougat) and newer.
How do I get an account?
Students can register from the sign-in screen. Staff accounts are set up for the institution. The role you pick at sign-in must match the role on your account.
Where does my data go?
Read the privacy policy — it explains what is handled, why, and how to ask for access or deletion.
