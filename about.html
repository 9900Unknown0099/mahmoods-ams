About

Built at SIT.
Built for class.

Mahmoods AMS began as a college project at Sharad Institute of Technology College of Engineering, Yadrav-Ichalkaranji — to make classroom attendance quicker, clearer and easier to trust.

The problem

Roll call hasn’t kept up with the classroom.

Lost teaching time

Calling out names can eat 5–10 minutes of every lecture.

Easy to get wrong

Manual registers and re-typed spreadsheets mean duplicate work and data-entry errors.

Late and early exits

A single roll call can’t tell who arrived late or left after being counted.

No live picture

Faculty and administrators can’t see who is present until the paperwork catches up.
